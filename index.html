rclone

A personal setup of rclone, the open-source file sync tool, used by Caleb Nanji to upload, sync and back up his own Google Drive from his own computer.

What it does

  • Copies files between folders on Caleb's computer and his Google Drive.
  • Shows upload progress on a local dashboard that runs only on that computer.

Who it's for

This app is for personal use only. It isn't offered to the public, and it has no accounts, sign-ups or servers of its own.
