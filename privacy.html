Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

This policy covers the Google app named "rclone" in the Google Cloud project run by Caleb Nanji. It's a personal setup of the open-source tool rclone, used only by Caleb with his own Google accounts.

What data the app accesses

With the permission you grant on Google's consent screen, the app reads, uploads, changes and deletes files in your Google Drive, only as instructed by commands run on your own computer. It doesn't access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

Where your data goes

  • Files move directly between your computer and Google's servers. They're never sent to the developer or to any other server.
  • The login token Google issues is stored only in the rclone configuration file on your own computer.
  • The app has no servers, database, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The developer has no way to see your files.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To remove the stored token from your computer, run rclone config delete <remote>.
